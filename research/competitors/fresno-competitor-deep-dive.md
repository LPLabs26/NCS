**High** for Hydrafacial and corrective-service competition.

## Competitive takeaways for NCS

### 1. NCS should own the lane of:
**luxury, feminine, results-driven, personalized skincare with Hydrafacial leadership**

### 2. NCS should avoid becoming:
- too broad and menu-heavy like a generic esthetics provider
- too medically cold like a correction-only clinic
- too discount-dependent like a convenience-first beauty service

### 3. Best strategic brand lane
NCS can sit between:
- **Sanctuary Beauty Co** (soft boutique luxury)
and
- **Polish & Peels** (advanced prestige authority)

That middle lane is strong:
- premium
- polished
- high-touch
- visible results
- easier to trust
- easier to book

### 4. Messaging to lean into now
- Hydrafacial as the signature glow + reset service
- custom facials as the no-guesswork entry point
- peels as expert-guided corrective care
- monthly skin maintenance as luxury discipline

### 5. Action items
1. create a stronger luxury brand story on Instagram and booking pages
2. use more proof-based content around glow, texture, and customized care
3. build a clearer custom-facial onboarding funnel
4. make peel content more educational and authority-led
5. keep urgency-based promos elegant, not discount-heavy
