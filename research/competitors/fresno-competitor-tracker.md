# Fresno Competitor Tracker

_Status: first-pass tracking sheet_

## How to use
Track 3-5 local Fresno esthetician / skincare brands and review them weekly for:
- offer positioning
- visual style
- posting cadence
- promotional tactics
- proof content (before/after, reviews, demos)
- booking incentives
