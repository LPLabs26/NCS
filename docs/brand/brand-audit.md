# NCS Aesthetics Brand Audit

_Date: 2026-03-27_

## Summary

NCS Aesthetics is best positioned as a **luxury, results-driven, high-touch skincare studio in Fresno** with Hydrafacial as the hero service and custom/corrective facials as the long-term retention engine.

## Core Brand Read

### Positioning
NCS Aesthetics helps clients achieve healthier, brighter, more refined skin through personalized esthetic treatments delivered in a premium but approachable setting.

### Vibe
- luxury but warm
- polished, feminine, elevated
- result-focused without feeling clinical or harsh
- personalized rather than one-size-fits-all

### Best-fit brand promise
- visible glow
- customized care
- premium treatment experience
- consistent skin maintenance

## Hero Offers

### Hydrafacial ladder
- Platinum Hydrafacial — $325 / 90 min
- Hydrafacial — $275 / 75 min
- Express Hydrafacial — $199 / 45 min
- package offers support retention and repeat visits

### Custom / corrective offers
- Custom Facial — $150 / 75 min
- Chemical Peel — $175+ / 45 min
- corrective / anti-aging facial tier around $200 / 90 min

## Strategic Opportunities

1. Market fewer hero offers more aggressively
2. Use Custom Facial as the first-time-client bridge offer
3. Position chemical peels as expert-guided corrective care
4. Build stronger transformation content around outcomes, not just menu items
5. Lean into monthly maintenance and skin-journey messaging

## Messaging Directions

### Hydrafacial
- glow reset
- decongest + hydrate
- event-ready skin
- monthly maintenance facial

### Custom Facial
- not sure what to book?
- personalized skin plan
- first facial / first visit entry point

### Chemical Peel
- texture refinement
- acne marks / discoloration support
- seasonal reset
- corrective skin strategy
