# Luxury Voice Guide

## Brand voice
NCS Aesthetics should sound:
- polished
- warm
- confident
- elevated
- personalized
- never desperate

## What the voice should feel like
- premium, but not cold
- expert, but not overly clinical
- feminine, but not childish
- inviting, but not generic

## Use more of
- refined
- glowing
- personalized
- rejuvenated
- refreshed
- results-driven
- curated
- elevated
- radiant
- tailored

## Use less of
- cheap
- deal
- hurry!!!
- last chance!!!
- flawless
- perfect skin
- miracle
- harsh medical jargon unless educational context requires it

## Caption style rules
- lead with a client problem, desire, or transformation
- keep sentences clean and readable
- avoid sounding overhyped
- end with a clear booking action

## Example tone
### Better
Your skin doesn’t need more guesswork — it may just need the right treatment. A customized facial can help reset, hydrate, and support your skin based on what it actually needs.

### Worse
Need glowing perfect skin ASAP?? Book now before spots run out!!!
