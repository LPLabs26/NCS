# Service Menu Optimization Recommendations

## Goal
Make the menu easier to market and easier for clients to choose from.

## Current strength
NCS already has a strong Hydrafacial ladder and enough service depth to support both glow-focused and corrective clients.

## Main risk
Too many service options can make first-time clients hesitate if the menu feels broad without guidance.

## Recommendations

### 1. Push fewer offers publicly
Feature these most often in marketing:
- Platinum Hydrafacial
- Hydrafacial
- Express Hydrafacial
- Custom Facial
- Chemical Peel

### 2. Use Custom Facial as the bridge offer
This should be the answer for clients who are unsure.

Suggested language:
If you’re not sure what to book, start with a custom facial so your treatment can be tailored to your skin’s current needs.

### 3. Keep add-ons in the booking flow, not the marketing spotlight
Add-ons are valuable, but they should not crowd the front-facing brand story.

### 4. Strengthen package visibility
NCS should talk more often about:
- maintenance
- consistency
- staying on track with skin goals
- package value without sounding discount-heavy

### 5. Consider naming / framing improvements later
Future option:
- Glow Reset Hydrafacial
- Signature Skin Reset
- Customized Skin Renewal Facial

Only do this if naming improves clarity rather than making the menu feel over-branded.

## Overall recommendation
Make the business feel curated. A luxury brand should feel easier to choose from, not harder.
