# Instagram Profile Optimization

## Goal
Make the Instagram profile convert more profile visits into inquiries and bookings.

## What the profile should communicate in seconds
1. what NCS does
2. who it helps
3. what to book first
4. why it feels premium and trustworthy

## Ideal profile structure
### Name field
Use searchable terms if possible.

Example:
**NCS Aesthetics | Fresno Esthetician**

This improves local discoverability while keeping the brand name clear.

### Bio goals
The bio should communicate:
- luxury skincare / facial specialty
- Circadia Pro authority
- location
- hero services
- booking direction

### Suggested bio direction
Fresno Esthetician | Hydrafacial • Circadia Pro • Waxing
Custom facials, peels, lashes + brows
Located inside La Dama Salon | North Fresno
Book your glow ↓

Alternative version:
Fresno esthetician specializing in Hydrafacials, Circadia Pro guidance, custom facials, and corrective skincare.
Luxury, high-touch treatments designed to support radiant skin.
Book below.

### Link strategy
The booking link should go directly to the strongest booking path.
If only one link is used, make sure it is the GlossGenius booking page.

## Profile photo guidance
The profile photo should feel:
- clean
- premium
- recognizable at small size
- either strong logo treatment or polished face-forward brand image

---

## Content pinning strategy
Pin 3 posts at the top.

### Recommended pinned posts
1. **Welcome / brand intro**
2. **Hydrafacial results or treatment explanation**
3. **Not sure what to book? Start here / Custom Facial guide**

This immediately answers the most important conversion questions.

---

## Highlight strategy
Highlights should reduce booking hesitation and increase trust.

### Recommended highlights
- New Here
- Hydrafacial
- Circadia Pro
- Facials
- Peels
- Results
- Reviews
- FAQs
- Book Now

### What each highlight should do
**New Here**
- intro to Natalie / NCS
- who NCS is for
- where to start

**Hydrafacial**
- benefits
- process
- who it’s for
- results

**Circadia Pro**
- what Circadia Pro means
- protect by day / repair by night education
- custom facial and peel-plan authority
- homecare guidance without public pricing

**Facials**
- custom facial education
- acne / glow / maintenance angles

**Peels**
- what peels help with
- who should ask about them
- aftercare expectations

**Results**
- before/after proof
- texture / glow / skin progress

**Reviews**
- testimonials
- trust signals

**FAQs**
- what to book
- downtime
- consultation guidance

**Book Now**
- direct how to book
- what to choose if unsure

---

## Profile content rules
- keep visual identity bright, clean, and elevated
- use fewer but stronger messages
- repeat hero offers often
- avoid making the page feel like a random beauty menu
- use Circadia to reinforce custom-treatment planning, peel education, barrier support, and homecare authority
- do not show Circadia retail product pricing publicly
- only use official Circadia assets with approved access and usage rights

## Most important profile principle
A new visitor should quickly understand:
- this is premium
- this is trustworthy
- this is for skin results
- I know what to book next
