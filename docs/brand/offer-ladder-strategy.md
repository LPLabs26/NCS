# Offer Ladder Strategy

## Goal
Create a clear path from first-time booking to long-term client retention.

## Level 1 - Entry point offers
### Purpose
Reduce hesitation and attract new clients.

### Best entry offers
- Custom Facial
- Express Hydrafacial
- Consultation

### Why
These are easier yeses for new clients who are unsure, price-aware, or simply need a starting point.

---

## Level 2 - Hero offers
### Purpose
Anchor the brand and drive premium bookings.

### Best hero offers
- Hydrafacial
- Platinum Hydrafacial

### Why
These are the strongest blend of luxury, visible results, and premium pricing. They should be the most visible in marketing.

---

## Level 3 - Corrective offers
### Purpose
Support results-focused clients and deeper skin goals.

### Best corrective offers
- Chemical Peel
- Anti-aging / corrective facials
- acne-focused care

### Why
These move the brand into expertise and long-term treatment planning.

---

## Level 4 - Retention offers
### Purpose
Increase lifetime value and repeat frequency.

### Best retention offers
- buy-3-get-1 Hydrafacial packages
- monthly maintenance messaging
- personalized recurring treatment recommendations

### Why
Retention creates stability, brand loyalty, and stronger client outcomes.

---

## Best marketing hierarchy
1. Hydrafacial / Platinum Hydrafacial
2. Custom Facial
3. Chemical Peels / corrective services
4. packages / maintenance reminders
5. lower-priority add-ons

## What to feature less often
- services that distract from the hero offers
- too many add-ons in front-facing marketing
- broad menu clutter without context

## Key rule
Clients should always understand the next best step.

That means the brand should consistently answer:
- new here? -> book a custom facial
- want glow? -> book a Hydrafacial
- want correction? -> ask about peels / treatment planning
- want maintenance? -> stay consistent and pre-book
