# Content Production SOP

## Goal
Create content consistently without making the process chaotic.

## Weekly content workflow
### Step 1 - Plan
- choose weekly hero service
- choose 3-5 main content pieces
- choose supporting story angles

### Step 2 - Script
- draft hooks
- write captions
- define CTA
- decide what visuals are needed

### Step 3 - Capture
- film treatment clips
- capture before/after visuals where appropriate
- capture relaxing room details and process shots

### Step 4 - Build
- edit reels
- assemble carousels
- prepare story slides
- finalize captions

### Step 5 - Publish
- publish according to content calendar
- use stories to support main post
- answer responses quickly

### Step 6 - Review
- note strongest hook
- note what drove replies or bookings
- save winning ideas as repeatable assets

## Core rule
Do not chase random trends if they do not support bookings, trust, or brand positioning.
