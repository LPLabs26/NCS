# Presentation Notes

## How to explain the strategy simply
NCS already has the services. The opportunity now is to make the business easier to understand, easier to book, and easier to repeat.

## Key framing lines
- We are not trying to add noise. We are trying to create clarity.
- The goal is to make premium care feel simple and trustworthy.
- Hydrafacial should be the hero, Custom Facial should be the bridge, and Peels should be the authority offer.
- The dashboard matters because clarity creates better decisions.

## Key message
Consistency will likely outperform complexity.
