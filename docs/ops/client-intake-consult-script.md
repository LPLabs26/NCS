# Client Intake / Consult Script

## Goal
Make first-time consultations and custom facial bookings feel premium, personal, and clear.

## Opening
Hi love, before we begin I want to learn a little more about your skin, your goals, and what’s been bothering you most so we can make sure you’re in the right treatment.

## Questions to ask
- What are your main skin concerns right now?
- What are you hoping to improve most?
- Have you had facials, Hydrafacials, or peels before?
- Is your skin sensitive or reactive?
- Are you currently using any active products at home?
- Do you have an event or timeline you’re preparing for?

## Guidance language
### If client is unsure
Based on what you’re describing, a custom facial is a great place to start because it allows us to tailor everything to what your skin needs right now.

### If client wants glow / reset
A Hydrafacial would likely be a beautiful fit if your goal is glow, hydration, and a deep cleanse.

### If client wants correction
If your focus is more on texture, post-breakout marks, or deeper corrective goals, we can talk about a peel or a longer-term treatment plan.

## Close
I want to make sure you leave feeling clear on the best next step for your skin. From here, I’d recommend [service] based on your goals.
