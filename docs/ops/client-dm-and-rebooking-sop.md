# Client DM and Rebooking SOP

## Objective
Maintain a luxury, responsive, high-touch client experience in DMs and after appointments.

## DM tone
- warm
- polished
- concise
- helpful
- never pushy

## DM response principles
1. answer the question clearly
2. reduce friction
3. guide to the right booking path
4. keep the tone elevated and easy

## Common DM situations

### 1. "What should I book?"
**Response template:**
If you’re unsure where to start, I usually recommend booking a custom facial so your treatment can be tailored to your skin’s current needs. If you’re mainly wanting a deep cleanse, hydration, and glow, a Hydrafacial is also a beautiful option.

### 2. "Do you have openings?"
**Response template:**
Yes — the best way to view current availability is through the booking link. If you’d like, I can also point you toward the best treatment option based on your skin goals.

### 3. "What helps with acne / texture / dullness?"
**Response template:**
That can depend on your skin and how sensitive it is, but a custom facial, Hydrafacial, or corrective treatment plan may all be helpful depending on your goals. If you want the most tailored option, start with a custom facial or consultation.

## Rebooking guidance
Rebooking language should feel supportive, not salesy.

### Good rebooking prompts
- To maintain your results, I’d recommend staying consistent with your treatments.
- If you’d like to keep your skin on track, booking your next appointment now is a great idea.
- Monthly maintenance is a great rhythm for many clients wanting to maintain glow and clarity.

## Aftercare follow-up template
Hi love — just checking in to see how your skin is feeling after your treatment. Keep following your aftercare instructions, stay hydrated, and let me know if you have any questions at all.

## Post-visit rebooking template
It was so good seeing you today. If you’d like to stay consistent with your results, I recommend booking your next appointment before the schedule fills in.

## Rules
- never over-message
- never guilt the client
- never sound rushed or transactional
- always make the next step feel easy
