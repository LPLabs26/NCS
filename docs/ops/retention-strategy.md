# Retention Strategy

## Goal
Increase repeat bookings and lifetime value while keeping the client experience high-touch and premium.

## Core retention levers
1. pre-book the next appointment
2. recommend a treatment rhythm
3. use package logic naturally
4. follow up after treatment
5. educate clients on consistency

## Recommended rhythms
### Hydrafacial clients
- every 4-6 weeks depending on goals

### Custom Facial clients
- every 4-6 weeks depending on goals and skin condition

### Peel / corrective clients
- based on treatment plan and skin tolerance

## Best rebooking moments
- immediately after treatment
- same-day follow-up
- when client compliments their results

## Messaging themes
- maintain your glow
- stay consistent with your skin goals
- your skin responds best to routine support
- great skin usually comes from consistency, not one appointment

## Retention opportunities
- monthly glow reset campaign
- pre-booking reminder at checkout
- package education in stories and captions
- before/after progress storytelling

## Key principle
Retention should feel like guidance, not pressure.
