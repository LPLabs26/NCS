# Booking Gap-Fill SOP

## Objective
Turn same-week or same-day availability into bookings without making the brand feel discount-driven.

## When to use
- unexpected cancellation
- open block in a prime time slot
- soft booking day
- same-week underfilled schedule

## Best promotion angles
1. limited opening
2. same-week glow appointment
3. self-care reminder
4. event-ready skin
5. monthly maintenance reminder

## Preferred framing
Use:
- exclusive
- available this week
- last opening this week
- perfect time for your glow reset
- if your skin has been needing attention, this is your sign

Avoid:
- desperate discount language
- overusing flash sale positioning
- multiple offers at once

## Story structure
### Slide 1
This week’s opening just became available.

### Slide 2
Perfect for a Hydrafacial / custom facial / glow reset.

### Slide 3
Book now before it’s gone.

## Feed / caption version
A rare opening just became available this week for anyone needing a skin reset, fresh glow, or some well-deserved self-care. If your skin has been feeling dull, congested, or overdue for attention, this is the perfect time to book.

## CTA
- DM to claim it
- Book through the link
- Reserve your appointment now

## Offer guidance
Use discounts sparingly.
Preferred order:
1. urgency only
2. add-on bonus
3. soft incentive
4. discount only if needed to fill a weaker slot
