# Rebooking at Checkout Script

## Goal
Encourage consistent care without sounding salesy.

## Base script
To maintain your results and keep your skin on track, I’d recommend staying consistent with your treatments. If you’d like, we can reserve your next appointment now so you have your ideal spot.

## Hydrafacial version
Hydrafacials work beautifully as part of a consistent glow and maintenance routine. Booking your next one now is the best way to stay on track.

## Custom Facial version
Because your skin can change over time, staying consistent with customized care is one of the best ways to support your results.

## Peel / corrective version
With corrective treatments, consistency matters even more. If you want to stay on track with your goals, it’s a great idea to reserve your next appointment now.

## Tone rules
- calm
- confident
- helpful
- never pushy
