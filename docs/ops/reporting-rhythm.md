# Reporting Rhythm

## Daily
- log bookings
- log leads / DMs
- note openings
- note content posted

## Weekly
- summarize wins and bottlenecks
- review strongest content and offers
- decide what to repeat next week
- decide which service needs more support

## Monthly
- identify top-performing campaign theme
- identify best-performing service category
- review retention signals
- refine content strategy for next month

## Rule
Do not just collect numbers. Every review should end with a next move.
