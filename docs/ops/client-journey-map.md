# Client Journey Map

## Goal
Design a smoother path from awareness to retention for NCS Aesthetics clients.

## Stage 1 - Awareness
### How clients discover NCS
- Instagram Reels
- Stories
- referrals
- local search / GlossGenius
- word of mouth

### Best content at this stage
- glow results
- treatment education
- before/after proof
- esthetician trust and personality

### Objective
Make NCS look premium, trustworthy, and easy to book.

---

## Stage 2 - Interest
### What the client is thinking
- What should I book?
- Will this help my skin?
- Is it worth the price?
- Do I trust this esthetician?

### Best tools at this stage
- custom facial positioning
- FAQs
- service sales scripts
- DM guidance
- educational posts

### Objective
Reduce hesitation and remove confusion.

---

## Stage 3 - Booking
### Key friction points
- too many choices
- uncertainty about best treatment
- price resistance
- fear of booking the wrong service

### Best conversion path
1. unsure client -> Custom Facial
2. glow-seeking client -> Hydrafacial
3. corrective / texture / acne marks client -> Peel consult or corrective plan

### Objective
Guide people toward the easiest right next step.

---

## Stage 4 - Experience
### What matters most
- premium treatment experience
- visible results
- personalized care
- aftercare confidence
- emotional trust

### Best support
- polished treatment room experience
- clear aftercare instructions
- calm confidence in service delivery
- follow-up check-in message

### Objective
Create a strong emotional and visible result so clients want to return.

---

## Stage 5 - Rebooking
### Best rebooking triggers
- at checkout / immediately after treatment
- within 24-48 hours via message
- when the client expresses satisfaction with results

### Best rebooking language
- maintain your results
- stay consistent with your skin goals
- keep your glow on track

### Objective
Turn one appointment into a treatment rhythm.

---

## Stage 6 - Retention
### Retention path examples
- Hydrafacial -> monthly maintenance
- Custom Facial -> personalized recurring care
- Peel -> corrective treatment series

### Tools that improve retention
- package offers
- pre-booking next appointment
- educational reminders
- monthly skin reset messaging
- seasonal treatment campaigns

### Objective
Move clients from occasional booking to consistent care.

---

## Highest-impact insight
NCS should not just sell facials. It should sell:
- a skin journey
- personalized guidance
- consistent maintenance
- luxury self-care with visible results
