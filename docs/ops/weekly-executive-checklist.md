# Weekly Executive Checklist

## Monday
- review upcoming bookings and likely slow windows
- choose the week’s hero offer
- review content plan for the week
- confirm stories, reels, and promotional angles

## Tuesday
- publish education-led content
- review DMs and inquiry patterns
- note recurring objections or booking hesitation

## Wednesday
- post social proof or treatment result content
- check whether same-week openings need a gap-fill post
- review engagement on first half of week’s content

## Thursday
- push conversion content
- post booking reminder / availability story
- draft weekend self-care angle

## Friday
- summarize what content performed best
- identify next week’s repeatable hooks
- review inquiries, bookings, and missed opportunities

## Weekly questions
1. Which offer got the most attention?
2. Which post format performed best?
3. What questions came up most in DMs?
4. What content felt most premium and on-brand?
5. What should be repeated next week?
