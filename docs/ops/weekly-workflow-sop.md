# Weekly Workflow SOP

## Monday - Planning
- review upcoming bookings
- identify open windows
- pick weekly hero service
- choose 3 main content pieces
- note follow-ups needed

## Tuesday - Education
- publish authority or FAQ content
- answer DMs
- update lead tracker

## Wednesday - Conversion
- publish social proof or treatment result content
- review whether openings need a gap-fill push
- follow up with warm leads

## Thursday - Offer push
- publish booking-oriented content
- post availability / urgency stories
- prep weekend self-care angle

## Friday - Review + retention
- note best-performing content
- review rebooking opportunities
- log weekly lessons
- decide what to repeat next week

## Ongoing rules
- protect the premium tone
- market fewer offers more consistently
- lead with guidance, not pressure
- use urgency sparingly but intentionally
