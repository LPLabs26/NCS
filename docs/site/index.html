FAQ

Simple answers before you book

Which service should I start with if I am new?

Hydrafacial is the safest starting point for most new clients because it is easy to understand, visibly refreshing, and helps establish the right next step.

What if I am not sure what to book?

Custom facial is the best bridge offer when you want a personalized treatment without guessing your way through the menu.

When do peels make the most sense?

Peels make more sense when the goal is corrective care, especially for texture, congestion, and tone refinement. The best option depends on your skin and timing.
