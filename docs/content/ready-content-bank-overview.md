# Ready Content Bank Overview

This content bank expands the approved NCS daily publishing system into a larger production-ready library:

- **Morning:** Daily Tips
- **Midday:** Hydrafacial
- **Evening:** Circadia hero posts

It is built for speed, consistency, and quality control.

## What this bank includes
- a larger batch of post packages for each daypart
- multiple hook options instead of one repetitive opener
- on-screen text that can be used directly in reels, carousels, or story sets
- caption drafts that already sound aligned with the NCS voice
- CTAs that support bookings without making every post feel pushy
- asset direction notes so filming and editing are faster

## Files
- `daily-tips-batch-pack.md`
- `hydrafacial-midday-batch-pack.md`
- `circadia-evening-hero-pack.md`
- `production-batch-structure.md`

## Packaging rules
Each package follows the same structure:
1. **Primary angle**
2. **Best format**
3. **Hook options**
4. **On-screen text**
5. **Caption**
6. **CTA options**
7. **Asset direction**

## Practical use
- pull 5 to 7 pieces at a time for weekly approval
- keep morning lighter and faster
- keep midday educational and treatment-forward
- give the strongest visuals and storytelling to evening Circadia
- rotate CTAs so the feed stays elegant and non-spammy

## Recommended approval rhythm
- approve morning in batches of 7 to 14
- approve midday in batches of 5 to 10
- review evening hero posts one by one or in smaller premium batches

## Quality guardrails
- avoid sounding clinical or salesy
- avoid repeating the exact same Hydrafacial promise every day
- keep Daily Tips simple enough to absorb in under 10 seconds
- make Circadia feel like expert-guided ritual, not product pushing
- use elegant restraint in all caption writing
