# 30-Day Content Plan

## Goal
Create a month of strategic Instagram content for NCS Aesthetics focused on:
- increasing bookings
- strengthening luxury positioning
- educating first-time clients
- creating repeat demand for Hydrafacials, Custom Facials, and Chemical Peels

## Posting rhythm
- 3 Reels per week
- 2 Carousels or static educational posts per week
- Daily Stories
- 1 social proof / testimonial angle per week
- 1 urgency / availability angle per week
