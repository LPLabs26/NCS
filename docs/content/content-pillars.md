# NCS Aesthetics Content Pillars

## 1. Luxury Glow / Results
Purpose: Show visible outcomes and premium treatment experience.

Examples:
- post-facial glow reveals
- dewy skin close-ups
- before/after texture improvements
- event-ready skin transformations

## 2. Education / Authority
Purpose: Build trust and reduce booking hesitation.

Examples:
- what Hydrafacial actually does
- peel myths vs facts
- who should book a custom facial
- what to expect after treatment

## 3. Personalized Care
Purpose: Emphasize custom treatment planning and high-touch service.

Examples:
- consultation snippets
- matching treatments to skin concerns
- why every client should not book the same service
- behind-the-scenes product/treatment selection

## 4. Social Proof
Purpose: Convert trust into bookings.

Examples:
- client testimonials
- repeat client routines
- skin journey progress updates
- common questions answered using real client scenarios

## 5. Fresno Luxury Lifestyle
Purpose: Root the brand locally and make it feel aspirational but accessible.

Examples:
- appointment day rituals
- treatment room ambiance
- self-care reminders for local clients
- seasonal skin needs in Fresno / Central Valley conditions
