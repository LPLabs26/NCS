# Mini-Movie 04 — Red Carpet Timeline

## Core concept
A fast, polished mini-movie built around event prep psychology. It reframes last-minute panic into a calm, luxury game plan and positions Hydrafacial as the trusted pre-event booking.

## Conversion goal
Capture clients preparing for weddings, birthdays, vacations, photoshoots, and special events.

## Best offer to feature
Hydrafacial / Platinum Hydrafacial

## Hook
If you have an event coming up, your glow should be on the calendar too.

## Story arc
- Trigger: upcoming event pressure
- Plan: ideal timing for treatment before photos or celebration
- Proof: smooth, hydrated, polished final look
- Action: reserve the event-ready appointment now

## Shot list
1. Close-up of calendar page or phone calendar marked “Event”
2. Beauty prep montage: jewelry, dress fabric, heels, makeup bag, invitation, or vacation bag
3. Cut to treatment bed prep at NCS
4. Hydrafacial clips with clean, luminous skin detail
5. Finishing touches: lip balm, hair tuck, mirror check
6. Final reveal in natural light with dressed-up or polished look
7. Overlay of timeline suggestion: 5–7 days before event
8. End card with booking CTA

## On-screen text
- Frame 1: Big event coming up?
- Frame 2: Do not leave your skin to chance
- Frame 3: Hydrafacial = smooth, hydrated, photo-ready skin
- Frame 4: Ideal timing: about 5–7 days before
- Frame 5: Fresh glow, polished finish
- Frame 6: Reserve your event-ready treatment
