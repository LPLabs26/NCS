# NCS Mini-Movies

Five storyboarded mini-movie concepts created for NCS Aesthetics, aligned to the current luxury warm-tone brand direction, Hydrafacial and Circadia positioning, and conversion-focused content goals.

## Included concepts
1. `01-golden-hour-glow-reset.md` — Hydrafacial hero reel for first-time and event-prep bookings
2. `02-the-skin-consult-scene.md` — Custom Facial authority reel for unsure first-time clients
3. `03-circadia-after-dark.md` — Circadia education reel to support homecare trust and add-on sales
4. `04-red-carpet-timeline.md` — Event-prep Hydrafacial reel for weddings, trips, and photo moments
5. `05-the-monthly-ritual.md` — Retention reel for rebooking and maintenance positioning

## Shared creative direction
- Warm, luxury, skin-close visuals
- Polished but inviting tone
- Clear booking next step in every concept
- Strong fit for Instagram Reels with Story support
