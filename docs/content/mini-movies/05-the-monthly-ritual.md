# Mini-Movie 05 — The Monthly Ritual

## Core concept
A luxurious retention-focused mini-movie that makes monthly facials feel like both a skin strategy and a personal standard. This should feel calm, aspirational, and quietly persuasive.

## Conversion goal
Increase rebooking and package interest for long-term clients.

## Best offer to feature
Monthly Hydrafacial maintenance / package / recurring facial plan

## Hook
The best skin results usually do not come from one random appointment.

## Story arc
- Truth: consistency creates visible change
- Ritual: monthly facial as part of elevated self-care
- Benefit: glow, texture support, hydration, momentum
- Action: rebook before you leave

## Shot list
1. Opening visual of calm room reset: towels, warm light, clean tray, robe detail
2. Client settling into the bed with a relaxed exhale
3. Montage of repeated treatment details: cleanse, exfoliation, hydration, finishing care
4. Slow luxury inserts: tea, product shelf, treatment room textures, linens
5. Close-up of glowing skin after service
6. Front desk or booking screen showing next appointment being set
7. Lifestyle-style exit shot: client walking out polished and relaxed
8. Final branded frame with monthly ritual message

## On-screen text
- Frame 1: Want better skin long term?
- Frame 2: Consistency matters more than guessing
- Frame 3: Monthly facials support glow + texture + hydration
- Frame 4: Luxury is a routine, not a one-time moment
- Frame 5: Keep your results moving forward
- Frame 6: Rebook your next treatment before you leave

## Voiceover / caption copy
### Voiceover option
Great skin usually is not the result of one random facial here and there. It comes from consistency, the right treatment plan, and giving your skin regular support. That is why I always talk about maintenance. Monthly facials help keep your glow going, support hydration and texture, and make it easier to build toward bigger skin goals over time. If you want results that feel intentional, this is the rhythm.

### Caption option
A facial can absolutely give you a beautiful reset.

But if you want skin that keeps improving, consistency is where the real magic happens.

Monthly treatments help support hydration, glow, smoother texture, and overall skin confidence over time. They also make your skincare feel less reactive and more intentional.

The easiest way to stay consistent? Rebook before life gets busy again.

## CTA
Rebook your next treatment before you leave.

## Posting notes
- Best for: Reel, 20–30 seconds with slower, luxe pacing
- Music: calm cinematic or soft R&B-inspired instrumental
- Visual palette: cream, toffee, bronze, warm ivory, low contrast glow
- Best audience: existing clients, repeat viewers, people who already engaged with Hydrafacial content
- Best supporting Story sequence: “Monthly maintenance clients know” text slide + booking link
- Comment prompt: “Are you on a monthly skin routine yet?”
- Conversion note: perfect for package mentions, membership framing, or reminder campaigns at checkout
