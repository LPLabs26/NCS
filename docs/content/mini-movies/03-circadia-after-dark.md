# Mini-Movie 03 — Circadia After Dark

## Core concept
A moody, luxurious education reel that uses the day-versus-night Circadia positioning to make homecare feel premium, intentional, and necessary. The tone should feel editorial, like a beauty campaign with expert guidance.

## Conversion goal
Increase trust in professional skincare recommendations and create add-on retail interest tied to treatment bookings.

## Best offer to feature
Circadia-supported facial experience + homecare guidance

## Hook
Your skin does not need the same thing all day and all night.

## Story arc
- Insight: skin has different needs by day and night
- Authority: Circadia supports the skin rhythm concept
- Experience: products chosen with precision, not random layering
- Action: ask for a tailored routine with your next treatment

## Shot list
1. Golden late-afternoon light over product lineup
2. Close-up of Circadia packaging in warm shadows
3. Applying daytime support product with text overlay about protection
4. Cut to darker, evening-toned frame with richer serum texture
5. Slow-motion shot of nighttime product application or dropper detail
6. Treatment room shelf styling with elevated, minimal product curation
7. Esthetician selecting products after a facial
8. Final frame with product + glowing post-facial skin

## On-screen text
- Frame 1: Your skin works in cycles
- Frame 2: Day = protect
- Frame 3: Night = repair
- Frame 4: Circadia is built around that rhythm
- Frame 5: Better support. Better consistency.
- Frame 6: Ask about your customized homecare plan

## Voiceover / caption copy
### Voiceover option
One of the reasons I love Circadia is that it supports the skin differently during the day and at night. During the day, your skin needs protection. At night, it shifts into repair mode. When your routine is built around that rhythm, your skincare starts making a lot more sense. That is why professional recommendations matter. It is not about using more. It is about using what actually fits your skin and when it needs it.

### Caption option
Luxury skincare is not just about what feels beautiful. It is about what makes sense for your skin.

Circadia is one of my favorite ways to support clients because it respects the skin’s natural rhythm — protection by day, repair by night.

If you have been layering random products and hoping for the best, this is your reminder that a more refined routine usually gets better results.

## CTA
Ask about your customized homecare plan at your next appointment.

## Posting notes
- Best for: Reel, 20–30 seconds or elevated product B-roll montage
- Music: editorial, sleek, slow-build beauty soundtrack
- Visual palette: amber shadows, candlelit warmth, deep neutrals, gold highlights
- Best pairing: post the same day as a Story sticker asking “Need help with your routine?”
- Comment prompt: “Would you want a day vs night routine breakdown?”
- Conversion note: strongest when paired with treatment footage so retail feels like part of a complete results plan, not a hard sell
