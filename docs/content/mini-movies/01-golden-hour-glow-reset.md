# Mini-Movie 01 — Golden Hour Glow Reset

## Core concept
A cinematic before-to-after style reel that positions Hydrafacial as the luxury reset for skin that looks tired, dull, or stressed. The mood should feel warm, sunlit, polished, and immediately aspirational.

## Conversion goal
Drive first-time and event-prep Hydrafacial bookings.

## Best offer to feature
Hydrafacial / Platinum Hydrafacial

## Hook
Your skin can look more refreshed in one appointment than after weeks of guessing at home.

## Story arc
- Problem: skin looks flat, tired, dehydrated
- Process: elevated, step-by-step Hydrafacial experience
- Payoff: lit-from-within glow reveal
- Action: book the glow reset now

## Shot list
1. Close-up of bare skin in warm natural light, minimal makeup, neutral expression
2. Slow pan of the treatment bed, folded towel, gold-toned room details, ambient lamp glow
3. Cleanse application in close detail
4. Hydrafacial wand gliding across the skin with serum sheen visible
5. Tight shot of hydration infusion / finishing steps
6. Relaxation moment with eyes closed, hands tucked, peaceful luxury feel
7. Final reveal turning toward window light with luminous skin
8. Closing frame on booking screen, front desk detail, or brand logo over glow shot

## On-screen text
- Frame 1: Dull skin? Start here.
- Frame 2: One treatment. Visible refresh.
- Frame 3: Deep cleanse + exfoliation + hydration
- Frame 4: Luxury care, tailored to your skin
- Frame 5: The glow reset everyone asks about
- Frame 6: Book your Hydrafacial at NCS Aesthetics
