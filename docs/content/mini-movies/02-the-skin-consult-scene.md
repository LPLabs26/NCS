# Mini-Movie 02 — The Skin Consult Scene

## Core concept
A warm, authority-building mini-movie that shows how NCS personalizes treatment decisions instead of pushing random services. This positions the Custom Facial as the easiest starting point for unsure clients.

## Conversion goal
Reduce hesitation for first-time clients who do not know what to book.

## Best offer to feature
Custom Facial

## Hook
Not sure what to book? You do not need to know before you come in.

## Story arc
- Problem: overwhelmed by too many skincare options
- Guidance: esthetician-led consultation and thoughtful product selection
- Reassurance: customized plan, no pressure, no guesswork
- Action: start with a custom facial

## Shot list
1. Client entering the suite or sitting down for consultation
2. Natalie reviewing skin concerns up close in flattering natural light
3. Close shot of products being selected with intention
4. Texture shots of Circadia products on tray or in hand
5. Gentle cleansing or treatment prep visual
6. Relaxing facial moment with warm linens and slow movement
7. Natalie speaking softly to camera or client, pointing out tailored plan
8. End card with service recommendation: Custom Facial

## On-screen text
- Frame 1: Not sure what to book?
- Frame 2: Start with a personalized plan
- Frame 3: Your skin concerns matter
- Frame 4: Texture, hydration, breakouts, sensitivity, glow goals
- Frame 5: No guesswork. No generic treatment.
- Frame 6: Start with a Custom Facial at NCS Aesthetics

## Voiceover / caption copy
### Voiceover option
If you are unsure what your skin actually needs, that is completely normal. You do not need to figure it out alone before booking. At NCS Aesthetics, we start by looking at your skin, your goals, and what will make the most sense for you right now. For many first-time clients, the best place to begin is a custom facial designed around what your skin needs today, not what a trend told you to book.

### Caption option
You do not need to know the perfect service before you book.

If you are stuck between glow, hydration, breakouts, sensitivity, texture, or just wanting your skin to feel better again, start with a Custom Facial.

This gives us space to personalize the treatment, support your current concerns, and guide you toward the right next step without the pressure of choosing blindly.

## CTA
Start with a custom facial.

## Posting notes
- Best for: Reel, 25–35 seconds
- Music: soft luxe instrumental or subtle piano + beat
- Visual palette: warm beige, caramel, skin-close textures, elevated calm
- Ideal audience: first-time clients, hesitant bookers, people engaging with educational content
- Best supporting Story sequence: question box “What are you trying to fix with your skin right now?”
- Comment prompt: “Want me to make a Hydrafacial vs Custom Facial version too?”
- Conversion note: this piece works well pinned or boosted because it answers a top booking objection
