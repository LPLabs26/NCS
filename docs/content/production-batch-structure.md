# Production Batch Structure

Use this structure to turn the content bank into weekly production without overcomplicating the workflow.

## Weekly build model
Each week should ideally contain:
- **5 to 7 Morning Daily Tips**
- **4 to 6 Midday Hydrafacial posts**
- **3 to 5 Evening Circadia hero posts**

That creates a healthy volume without making the content feel repetitive.

## Content mix by daypart

### Morning Daily Tips
**Purpose:** fast value, trust-building, daily visibility

**Preferred formats:**
- 3-frame story set
- simple talking story
- single-slide tip graphic
- ultra-short reel

**Production standard:**
- low lift
- one idea only
- soft visual polish
- quick approval

### Midday Hydrafacial
**Purpose:** treatment visibility, objections, consideration, rebooking support

**Preferred formats:**
- short reel
- FAQ carousel
- treatment myth post
- before/after style education

**Production standard:**
- clear benefit focus
- one treatment angle at a time
- simple proof language
- direct but calm CTA

### Evening Circadia
**Purpose:** strongest post of day, brand elevation, homecare authority, retention

**Preferred formats:**
- premium reel
- cinematic b-roll reel
- polished carousel
- ritual-led talking video

**Production standard:**
- strongest hook
- strongest visuals
- strongest copy
- strongest packaging

## Weekly approval packet template
For each weekly packet include:

### Packet cover
- week name
- production date
- approval status
- filming needs
- missing assets

### Morning section
For each morning post include:
- title
- format
- hook
- on-screen text
- CTA
- footage needed

### Midday section
For each midday post include:
- title
- format
- angle
- hook
- caption
- CTA
- visual proof needed

### Evening section
For each evening hero include:
- title
- format
- hero message
- hook options
- full on-screen text sequence
- caption
- CTA options
- product / treatment visuals needed

## Suggested 4-week rotation

### Week 1
- barrier basics
- glow reset
- day vs night skin rhythm

### Week 2
- hydration habits
- event-ready Hydrafacial
- nighttime repair ritual

### Week 3
- SPF and pigmentation prevention
- congestion / deep cleanse education
- consistency and homecare support

### Week 4
- makeup prep / skin prep
- monthly maintenance message
- Circadia as luxury support system

## Asset capture list
Create one filming session that covers:
- clean vanity product shots
- Natalie consulting with client
- cleanse and treatment application close-ups
- extraction machine / Hydrafacial moments
- towels, robes, warm lighting, room reset
- Circadia products styled on tray
- hands opening product, applying product, texture close-ups
- post-treatment glow shots
- mirror check / client lifestyle details

## Editing notes
- keep text sparse and elegant
- use warm neutral color grading
- avoid loud transitions
- favor slow, confident pacing
- let skin texture and glow do the selling

## CTA rotation bank
Use these instead of repeating one CTA constantly:
- Book your appointment through the link in bio.
- DM us if you want help choosing the right treatment.
- Save this for your next skin reset.
- Ask us about your ideal treatment plan.
- Reserve your Hydrafacial this week.
- Ask about your evening homecare ritual.
- Start with a custom plan if you are unsure what to book.
- Rebook before you leave if consistency is your goal.
