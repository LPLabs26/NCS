## DT27 — If you are testing a new product, change one thing at a time
**Title:** If you are testing a new product, change one thing at a time  
**Category:** Daily Tips  
**Daypart:** Morning  
**Hook:** You cannot read your skin clearly if you change the whole conversation at once.

**On-screen text:**

4. Stop creating skincare confusion
