# Daily Content Cadence

NCS content should follow a 3-part daily rhythm instead of stuffing multiple moments into one post.

## Core rule
One post = one moment of the day + one emotional job + one CTA.

## Morning posts
Purpose:
- prepare
- protect
- glow
- confidence

Good themes:
- glow reset
- event prep
- hydration/protection
- openings and booking prompts

## Midday posts
Purpose:
- educate
- clarify
- guide
- reduce hesitation

Good themes:
- what to book
- treatment comparison
- myth busting
- why a treatment works

## Evening posts
Purpose:
- reset
- recover
- ritual
- repair

Good themes:
- nighttime skin rhythm
- recovery
- aftercare
- homecare
- maintenance / rebooking

## Team responsibilities
- Samir: sets daily theme and approves cadence
- Michael: creates the actual morning / midday / evening content pieces
- Patrice: enforces structural consistency and QA
- Peter: packages and schedules each daily trio
