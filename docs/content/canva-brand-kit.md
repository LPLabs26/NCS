# Canva Brand Kit For NCS Aesthetics

Use this as the design system for the first production batch of Instagram posts.

## Brand Direction

NCS should feel:

- educated
- warm
- direct
- elevated but approachable
- comfort-first
- local and personal

Avoid generic spa visuals, neon medspa aesthetics, cold clinical layouts, or overly salesy promo graphics.

## Core Palette

Pull the visual language from the scheduler so the app and the content feel like the same brand.

- Shell: `#F6EFE8`
- Soft cream: `#FBF7F3`
- Ink: `#1D1A17`
- Muted brown: `#6D635C`
- Terracotta accent: `#CA7C5C`
- Deep terracotta: `#91543B`
- Sage accent: `#8CA394`

Recommended Canva use:

- Backgrounds: shell, cream, soft gradient blends
- Headlines: ink
- Body copy: muted brown
- CTA pill: ink or deep terracotta
- Secondary accent: sage for educational posts and calm skin/barrier content

## Typography

Preferred pair:

- Display / headline: `Fraunces`
- Body / support: `Manrope`

If Fraunces is not available in Canva, use the closest editorial serif available in the workspace and keep the same hierarchy:

- strong serif headline
- clean sans-serif body

## Template Families

### 1. Text-led hero static

Use for:

- start here posts
- trust posts
- offer reminders
- consult posts

Rules:

- one dominant headline
- short supporting sentence
- one CTA pill
- lots of negative space

### 2. Educational carousel

Use for:

- SPF rules
- Hydrafacial tiers
- Circadia education
- wax prep

Rules:

- one idea per slide
- large numbers or short labels
- no paragraph-heavy slides
- final slide should always carry the CTA

### 3. Reel cover

Use for:

- Hydrafacial hooks
- lash lift
- Hydraback
- custom facial explainer

Rules:

- 3 to 7 words max in the main cover line when possible
- keep cover text bold and legible from thumbnail size
- use the same CTA style across all reel covers

### 4. Circadia hero

Use for:

- protect by day, repair by night
- custom facial planning
- barrier support
- peel consult education

Rules:

- slightly darker, richer mood than Hydrafacial posts
- use deep neutrals, amber, terracotta, espresso, and cream
- keep it advanced and polished, never medical-looking
- include the official Circadia logo lockup when the post is directly about Circadia

## Brand Logo Rule

If a post is directly about a product or brand partner, include the approved logo in the final Canva export.

For NCS right now:

- Circadia posts should include the official Circadia logo
- keep the logo clean, small, and intentional
- place it in a corner lockup, not as the main headline
- only use rights-cleared PNG or SVG files

Do not invent, redraw, or approximate a logo with typed text when the real mark is required.

## Photo And Video Rules

Use real NCS media first for:

- Natalie
- treatment-room details
- Hydrafacial machine clips
- towels, products, setup
- consult moments
- lash and brow work

Do not use AI-generated fake clients, fake before/after imagery, or fake treatment proof in place of real work.

## OpenAI Image Use

OpenAI-generated artwork is best for:

- abstract skincare backdrops
- editorial still-life concepts
- mood-building backgrounds
- ingredient or ritual atmosphere
- filler visuals when a post is mostly text-led

Do not use OpenAI-generated images for:

- fake client result photos
- before/after content
- intimate waxing visuals
- anything that could be mistaken for a real NCS client transformation

## Compliance Reminders

- Do not show Platinum Hydrafacial B3G1 pricing until owner confirms it.
- Do not show public Circadia retail product pricing.
- Do not use official Circadia assets unless usage rights are confirmed.
- Do not promote specific Circadia services unless the owner has confirmed they are offered.
- Do not use client reviews, before/after images, or identifiable client media without permission.
- Keep claim language safe: designed to, can help, supports, great for, may improve the look of, results vary.

## Export Sizes

Use:

- Feed static and carousel: `1080 x 1350`
- Reel cover: `1080 x 1920` source layout, safe crop for feed cover
- Stories: `1080 x 1920`
