# Service Sales Script Pack

## Hydrafacial inquiry
If your main goal is a deep cleanse, hydration, and that fresh polished glow, a Hydrafacial is a beautiful option. It’s one of the best services for skin that feels dull, congested, or in need of a reset.

## Custom Facial inquiry
If you’re not sure what to book, I usually recommend starting with a custom facial. It allows the treatment to be tailored to your skin’s current needs so you’re not guessing from the menu.

## Chemical Peel inquiry
If you’re wanting to focus more on texture, post-breakout marks, dullness, or corrective skin goals, a chemical peel may be a great option. The best place to start is with a consultation or a guided treatment plan.

## Price objection
I completely understand. The goal is to make sure you’re booking the treatment that gives you the most value for your skin goals, not just booking the most expensive option. If you’d like, I can help point you toward the best place to start.

## Hesitant first-time client
That’s completely normal. If you’re new and want the most personalized option, a custom facial is usually the easiest and most comfortable place to begin.

## Rebooking prompt
To keep your skin on track and maintain your results, I’d recommend staying consistent with your treatments. Booking your next appointment now is a great way to keep that momentum going.
