## MDT01 — Your skin does not need a punishment cleanse every morning
- **Title:** Your skin does not need a punishment cleanse every morning
- **Category:** Daily Tips
- **Daypart:** Morning
- **Hook:** If your skin feels tight by 9 a.m., your morning cleanse may be too harsh.
- **Caption:** A lot of people assume a stronger cleanse always means a better cleanse. Usually it just means your skin has to work harder to recover. In the morning, gentle is often the smarter choice, especially if your skin already feels dry, sensitive, or reactive.
- **On-screen text:**
  1. Morning tip
  2. Do not over-cleanse first thing
  3. Tight skin is not the goal
  4. Gentle support usually looks better later
- **CTA:** Save this for your next routine reset.
- **Visual direction:** Soft sink-side video, cleanser texture, lukewarm water, clean towel detail, natural window light.
- **Approval notes:** Safe educational post. No medical claims. Best if filmed in robe or neutral morning styling.
