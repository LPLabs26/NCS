# Content Pipeline Ops

## Goal
Move every idea from raw input to published post through a visible, enforceable system.

## Core pipeline
1. Idea captured
2. Qualified
3. Draft assigned
4. Draft ready
5. Awaiting approval
6. Approved
7. Scheduled
8. Posted
9. Reviewed
10. Repurposed or archived

## Stage definitions

### 1. Idea captured
Source may be:
- Natalie voice note or text
- FAQ from DMs
- treatment room insight
- testimonial
- booking gap
- seasonal moment
- trend
- prior winning post worth reworking

Required fields:
- raw idea
- source
- service tied to it
- urgency
- suggested format if obvious

### 2. Qualified
The planner decides:
- which pillar this belongs to
- what business goal it supports
- whether it is top, middle, or bottom-of-funnel
- whether it needs filming, design, proof, or copy only

Qualification questions:
- does this support bookings, trust, retention, or awareness?
- is this tied to a current offer or business need?
- is this worth making this week, this month, or later?

### 3. Draft assigned
Each content card gets:
- owner
- due date
- asset request
- approval target date
- publish target date

### 4. Draft ready
A draft is only ready when it includes:
- hook
- structure or shot list
- caption
- CTA
- asset notes
- expected publish slot

### 5. Awaiting approval
Must be complete and decision-ready. Use the approval workflow doc.

### 6. Approved
Approved items should immediately receive:
- final scheduled date
- platform notes
- any story support planned around them

### 7. Scheduled
Once scheduled, confirm:
- time
- thumbnail or cover
- CTA and booking path
- supporting stories if needed

### 8. Posted
After posting, log:
- URL or identifier
- posted date
- hero service
- campaign tag

### 9. Reviewed
Within 48 to 72 hours, log:
- reach
- saves
- shares
- replies / DMs
- clicks
- inquiries / bookings if known
- lessons

### 10. Repurposed or archived
If it worked, spin it into:
- story sequence
- carousel
- talking Reel
- FAQ answer
- caption variation

## Pipeline rules
- no post skips qualification
- no draft enters approval half-finished
- no approved post sits unscheduled more than 24 hours unless intentionally held
- every posted item gets a short performance review
- winning posts must be repurposed, not admired once and forgotten

## Priority logic
Rank content in this order:
1. supports current bookings or gap-fill needs
2. addresses repeated objections or FAQs
3. showcases proof for a hero service
4. strengthens retention or rebooking
5. supports long-term brand authority

## Minimum healthy pipeline
Always keep at least:
- 10 ideas captured
- 5 qualified cards
- 3 draft-ready posts
- 2 approved posts
- 3 days of stories planned ahead

## Weekly content mix target
- 1 authority / education post
- 1 trust / proof post
- 1 conversion-oriented post
- daily light story presence
- 1 retention or rebooking prompt
