# Recurring Routines

## Daily routine

### 8:00 AM - signal scan
Owner: strategist + planner

Check:
- today’s bookings and open windows
- yesterday’s post performance
- DMs, FAQs, and objections
- trend or seasonal hooks worth using

Output:
- one recommended content angle
- one booking-support action if needed
- updates to the queue

### 10:30 AM - production block
Owner: planner + producer

Do:
- convert raw ideas into drafts
- request missing assets from Natalie only if truly needed
- finish anything targeted for same-day approval

Output:
- new draft-ready posts
- updated content board

### 11:30 AM - approval batch
Owner: approval manager

Do:
- send Natalie a clean batch
- keep to 3 to 5 items
- include only decision-ready posts

Output:
- approved / revise / hold decisions

### 1:00 PM - scheduling + community block
Owner: scheduler + lead manager

Do:
- schedule approved content
- prep same-day stories
- answer warm comments and DMs
- route repeated objections back into idea queue

Output:
- scheduled items
- DM follow-up notes

### 4:30 PM - closeout
Owner: analyst + planner

Do:
- log today’s activity
- identify tomorrow’s biggest gap
- make sure next-day content exists

Output:
- end-of-day notes
- tomorrow ready list

## Weekly routine

### Monday - weekly direction
- choose hero service
- choose weekly goal: awareness, bookings, retention, or gap-fill
- build the weekly brief
- confirm minimum pipeline health

### Tuesday - authority day
- prioritize education or FAQ content
- log new objections and talking points

### Wednesday - proof + conversion day
- push testimonial, results, or treatment explanation content
- check if bookings need a midweek urgency push

### Thursday - offer / schedule support day
- publish content tied to openings, urgency, or next-week booking behavior
- prep weekend stories

### Friday - analysis + reset
- review metrics and bookings impact
- label repeat / stop / test
- build starting queue for next week
- batch any Monday-ready approvals

## Monthly routine

### Week 1
- review best-performing services and formats
- update content themes for the month

### Week 2
- refresh story sets, CTA language, and FAQ list

### Week 3
- repurpose top performers into 2 to 3 new assets

### Week 4
- monthly retro: what content moved bookings, what wasted effort, what Natalie should approve less often

## Natalie touchpoints
Natalie should usually only have four recurring touchpoints:
- idea drop whenever she has one
- daily approval batch when needed
- Monday high-level priority confirmation
- Friday or monthly quick review of wins and direction

If the system is healthy, Natalie is not in the weeds between those moments.
