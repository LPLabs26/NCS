# Approval Workflow

## Goal
Make Natalie’s role simple: review finished packages, approve fast, and only weigh in on true business decisions.

## Approval standard
Do not ask for approval unless the draft includes all of the following:
- final hook
- final caption
- CTA
- format noted (Reel, carousel, story, static)
- asset status
- proposed publish date
- reason this post matters now
- yes/no decision Natalie needs to make

If any of that is missing, the team is not ready to escalate.

## Approval lanes

### Lane 1: Auto-approved by rules
No Natalie review required if all are true:
- based on an already-approved content format
- no new offer or pricing claim
- no sensitive before/after or testimonial issue
- no off-brand language
- only minor caption, crop, or scheduling changes

### Lane 2: Natalie quick approval
Use for most feed posts and planned stories.

What Natalie sees:
- post title
- preview or summary
- caption
- publish date
- CTA
- buttons/options: approve / revise / hold

Expected turnaround:
- same day when sent before 3 PM
- next business morning if sent later

### Lane 3: Natalie decision required
Use when there is real judgment involved.

Examples:
- new promo angle
- limited-time offer
- pricing mention
- treatment claim that could create risk
- before/after content that feels too aggressive
- anything that changes brand positioning

What Natalie sees:
- decision needed
- recommended option
- backup option
- deadline
- consequence of delay

## Daily approval rhythm

### 11:30 AM approval batch
Send only if there are complete items.

Batch limit:
- 3 to 5 items max
- most urgent first

### 4:30 PM cleanup batch
Only use for time-sensitive next-day content or blocked items.

Avoid:
- one-off random pings
- sending half-baked drafts
- asking Natalie to rewrite copy herself

## Approval SLA
- strategist/planner prepares batch by 11:00 AM
- Natalie reviews within same business day when possible
- scheduler queues approved posts immediately
- revisions return within 24 hours
- blocked items escalate after 48 hours

## Escalation rules
Escalate when:
- a post misses two approval windows
- Natalie has not answered and the content is time-sensitive
- legal, ethical, or reputation risk is unclear
- business priorities changed midweek

Escalation format:
1. what is blocked
2. why it matters
3. best recommendation
4. latest safe publish time

## Approve / revise / hold definitions
- Approve: publish as proposed
- Revise: make the requested change and return in next batch
- Hold: not for now; keep in backlog or re-angle later

## What Natalie should never have to do
- write captions from scratch
- chase missing assets
- remember what stage a post is in
- explain the workflow repeatedly
- answer questions that the decision rules already cover
